TERMS OF SERVICE

Welcome to EzBridge! EzBridge is your free Universal Server that lets you build online projects at zero cost. You never have to pay real money—instead, you can use your earned EzCoins to expand your limits. Not only can you publish sites and host projects on EzBridge, but you can also chat with people, add friends, and even voice or video call them! Below are the rules you must follow while exploring and creating on EzBridge:

1 - Inappropriate Language
Offensive language, sexual content, and racism are strictly prohibited on EzBridge.
2 - Inappropriate Content
If your EzBridge project contains malicious scripts or harmful content, you may be banned for an indefinite amount of time.
3 - Exploiting Servers & Crashing Attempts
Attempting to lag or crash a server or other users can result in a ban lasting 2 weeks to 2 months, depending on severity.
4 - Doxxing or Leaking Confidential Info
Leaking someone's IP address, private information, or confidential secrets can result in a ban lasting more than a month.
5 - Exploiting EzBridge
Attempting to exploit within a user project (for example, bypassing server checks to cheat) can get you banned for up to a week. Attempting to exploit vulnerabilities in EzBridge's universal server can result in a permanent ban.
6 - Harassing Users
Applies to EzBridge private chats and global chats: deliberately harassing or disturbing other users can result in a ban from over a week up to a month. Please be respectful on EzBridge!
