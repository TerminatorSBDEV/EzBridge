TERMS OF SERVICE

Hello fellow user! I'd like to welcome you to my project, EzBridge. EzBridge is your free Universal Server that lets you build online projects at zero cost! You literally don't have to pay real money for anything—instead, you can use your earned EzCoins to expand your limits. Not only can you publish sites and host projects on EzBridge, but you can also chat with people, add friends, and even voice/video call them! Isn't that cool? This platform has even more features than what's listed here. Below are the rules you must follow while navigating and creating in EzBridge:

1 - Inappropriate Language
Offensive language, sexual content, and racism are strictly prohibited in EzBridge.
2 - Inappropriate Content
If your EzBridge project is caught containing malicious scripts or harmful content, you can be banned for an undefined amount of time.
3 - Exploiting Servers & Crashing Attempts
Attempting to lag or crash a server or other users can get you banned for a long time—typically around 2 weeks to 2 months, depending on the severity of your actions.
4 - "Doxxing" or Leaking Confidential Info
Leaking someone's IP address, private information, or secrets meant to remain confidential can result in a ban lasting more than a month.
5 - Exploiting EzBridge
Trying to exploit within a user project (for example, bypassing a server check to become immortal, killing everyone at once, or cheating) can get you banned for less than a week. However, attempting to find and exploit vulnerabilities in EzBridge's universal server can result in a permanent ban (or a multi-month suspension).
6 - Harassing / Annoying Users
Applies to EzBridge private chats and global chats: deliberately harassing, annoying, or creeping out someone to drive them off EzBridge can result in a ban from over a week up to a month. Please be respectful on EzBridge!
